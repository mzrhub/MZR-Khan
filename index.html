Vision

A productive generation, a stronger Pakistan

My aim is simple: make the young generation of Pakistan productive, and through that, make Pakistan's economy stronger.

Education vision

I want to transform Pakistan's education system with practical, advanced learning that matches international standards — helping students build academic and professional identities strong enough to compete globally.

Technology vision

I want to promote technology that makes life easier and more efficient, and to teach people how to use it responsibly for their own personal and professional growth.

Flagship initiative

RILS — Rehman International Learning System

My long-term mission is to replace an outdated, memorization-driven education model with RILS — a modern, practical, internationally benchmarked framework for how Pakistani students learn. It's the throughline connecting everything I build under MZR HUB.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MZR HUB?
MZR HUB is the digital ecosystem I founded, bringing together education, technology and skill-development ventures — NextInova Solutions & Skills College, ZEDEMY, and the upcoming ZOTIVA — under one mission for Pakistan's youth.
What is NextInova Solutions & Skills College?
It's the venture I lead as CEO, focused on practical digital solutions and structured skills training so students graduate with real, employable ability rather than theory alone.
What does ZEDEMY offer students?
ZEDEMY provides organised educational resources for Matric, Intermediate and Nursing students, along with timed online tests during exam season to help them prepare with confidence.
When will ZOTIVA launch?
ZOTIVA is currently in development. It will be announced through MZR HUB's official channels — including this site and my social media — once it's ready to go live.
What is RILS?
RILS, the Rehman International Learning System, is my long-term initiative to replace outdated, memorization-driven education with a modern, practical, internationally benchmarked way for Pakistani students to learn.
